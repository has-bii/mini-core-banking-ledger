The brief

A mobile ledger for operations staff and accountants. Equal mirrored halves and an open center express balance, precision and clarity.

Final design

Geometric Balanced M

Two mirrored halves form one balanced M, with a continuous central gap.

  • Angular forms and flat terminals give the mark a geometric character.
  • An outlined wordmark keeps the artwork independent of installed fonts.
  • A dedicated small-size cut opens the center for favicons.
